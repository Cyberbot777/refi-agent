# Streamline Government Refinance Checklist

**FHA Streamline Refinance and VA IRRRL (Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan)**

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Version | 1.0 |
| Effective Date | January 13, 2026 |
| Owner | Operations / Underwriting |
| Purpose | Standardize document and policy checks for Streamline Government refinances and support automation |

---

## How to Use This Checklist

Mark each item as **Verified** or **N/A** and record the evidence source (document name, page, or system record). If any required item fails, stop and issue conditions or mark **Ineligible** with the reason code in your LOS.

### Recommended Order of Operations

1. Package completeness and document readability
2. Program routing (FHA vs VA) and hard-stop eligibility
3. Seasoning and payment history
4. Borrower-change logic (FHA credit-qualifying determination)
5. Net Tangible Benefit / Recoupment calculations
6. Title/subordination, payoff, and escrow/insurance validations (can run in parallel)

## Section B - FHA Streamline Refinance Checklist

*Applies to FHA-insured mortgages being refinanced under the Streamline Refinance program (credit-qualifying or non-credit-qualifying).*

### B1 - Program Eligibility (Hard Stops)

| Checklist Item | Status |
|----------------|--------|
| **Existing loan is FHA-insured** and is the lien being refinanced (verify FHA case number/servicer evidence) | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **No cash-out**: incidental cash back at closing does not exceed $500 | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Loan is current** (not delinquent) at time of underwriting and expected to be current at closing | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Manual underwriting**: Streamline is underwritten to FHA Streamline requirements (not AUS/TOTAL findings) | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |

### B2 - Seasoning and Mortgage Payment History

| Checklist Item | Status |
|----------------|--------|
| **Seasoning**: borrower has made at least 6 payments on the mortgage being refinanced | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Seasoning**: at least 6 months have passed since the first payment due date of the mortgage being refinanced | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Seasoning**: at least 210 days have passed since the closing date of the mortgage being refinanced | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Payment history rule**: validate the most recent 12 months and confirm the policy requirement for late payments prior to FHA case number assignment (document the rule set/version used) | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Forbearance**: if prior forbearance applies, confirm it is completed and required post-forbearance payment history is satisfied per your policy | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |

### B3 - Borrowers and Credit-Qualifying Determination

| Checklist Item | Status |
|----------------|--------|
| **Borrower comparison**: compare borrowers on existing FHA note vs new loan | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Non-credit-qualifying allowed** only if all borrowers remain (or borrower removal is permitted by documented exception such as death/divorce and payment history criteria are met) | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Credit-qualifying required** if borrower additions/removals occur outside permitted exceptions | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Occupancy**: occupancy type (owner-occupied, approved secondary, or non-owner) is documented and permitted under your FHA Streamline policy | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |

### B4 - Required Documentation and File Integrity

| Checklist Item | Status |
|----------------|--------|
| **Case number**: FHA case number assigned and recorded | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **URLA**: abbreviated URLA permitted for non-credit-qualifying; full URLA required for credit-qualifying (per your policy) | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Credit-qualifying only**: credit report, income documentation, and debt verification collected and reviewed | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Title/subordination**: confirm any existing subordinate liens are properly subordinated and no new subordinate financing is added (unless permitted) | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Escrow**: taxes and insurance escrow established/updated as required; validate hazard insurance coverage and tax status | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |

### B5 - Net Tangible Benefit, Loan Amount, and Cash Back

| Checklist Item | Status |
|----------------|--------|
| **Net Tangible Benefit (NTB) test selected**: identify scenario (fixed-to-fixed, fixed-to-ARM, ARM-to-fixed, term reduction, etc.) | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **NTB calculation completed** using combined rate (note rate + annual MIP) and term change where applicable; attach calc output | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **NTB meets threshold** for the selected scenario under the policy version in effect (record old vs new combined rate, term, and payment) | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Loan amount calculation**: confirm base loan amount, financed items, and any UFMIP/MIP treatment per policy; confirm no impermissible costs are financed | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Cash-to-borrower**: confirm cash back at closing is within $500 maximum incidental cash back | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |

## Section C - VA IRRRL Checklist

*Applies to VA Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loans (IRRRLs) refinancing an existing VA-guaranteed loan.*

### C1 - Program Eligibility (Hard Stops)

| Checklist Item | Status |
|----------------|--------|
| **Existing loan is VA-guaranteed** and is the loan being refinanced; verify via VA records/loan file | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Same property**: IRRRL is secured by the same property as the loan being refinanced | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **No improper cash-out**: ensure loan amount/rounding avoids cash to the Veteran (follow VA Form 26-8923 and rounding guidance) | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Allowable costs only**: new loan includes only allowable fees/costs per VA guidance; document any discount points included | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |

### C2 - Seasoning and Payment History

| Checklist Item | Status |
|----------------|--------|
| **Seasoning test**: first payment due date is at least 210 days prior to IRRRL closing date | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Seasoning test**: at least 6 consecutive monthly payments have been made on the loan being refinanced | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Evidence**: payment history/ledger or other acceptable evidence retained in file | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |

### C3 - Net Tangible Benefit (NTB)

| Checklist Item | Status |
|----------------|--------|
| **NTB scenario identified**: fixed-to-fixed, fixed-to-ARM, or ARM refinance | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Fixed-to-fixed**: new interest rate is at least 0.50% lower than the rate being refinanced (unless exempt under VA guidance) | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Fixed-to-ARM**: new initial interest rate is at least 2.00% lower than the rate being refinanced and any applicable points/requirements are satisfied | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Payment change**: confirm P&I decreases unless permitted exception applies (ARM refinance, term reduction, energy improvements, etc.) | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |

### C4 - Fee Recoupment (Statutory 36-Month Test)

| Checklist Item | Status |
|----------------|--------|
| **Recoupment calculation completed**: statutory fee recoupment period is 36 months or less (exclude taxes, escrow deposits, and the VA funding fee from the recoupment numerator as required) | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Recoupment evidence retained**: final disclosure or separate calculation showing total fees/costs and monthly P&I reduction | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **If P&I is not reduced**: confirm the file meets VA guidance for those limited cases (e.g., term reduction) and that impermissible fees are not charged where prohibited | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |

### C5 - 20% Payment Increase Trigger

| Checklist Item | Status |
|----------------|--------|
| **PITI +20% trigger evaluated**: compare old vs new total monthly payment (PITI) | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **If PITI increases by 20% or more**: underwrite/verify ability to pay (income stability and obligations) and include lender certification that Veteran qualifies | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |

### C6 - Required Documentation and Disclosures

| Checklist Item | Status |
|----------------|--------|
| **VA Form 26-8923 completed** and retained (IRRRL Worksheet) and used to compute max loan amount | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Veteran statement and lender certification retained** showing old vs new rate/payment and recoupment timeframe | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Disclosures timing**: required comparison/recoupment disclosures delivered per VA guidance (document timing in file) | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |
| **Title/subordination**: confirm any subordinate liens are subordinated and do not violate IRRRL requirements | ☐ Verified ☐ N/A |

## Appendix - Automation Notes

This checklist is designed to be automation-friendly.

### Recommended Automated Gates

1. Package completeness
2. Extraction confidence
3. Hard-stop eligibility
4. Seasoning/payment history
5. Borrower-change routing (FHA)
6. NTB and (VA) recoupment calculations
7. Parallel validations (title/payoff/escrow)
8. Final decision and audit log persistence (rule outputs + evidence links + policy version)

### Key Data Elements to Capture

| Category | Elements |
|----------|----------|
| **Program** | FHA Streamline or VA IRRRL |
| **Property** | Address, type |
| **Borrowers** | Old vs new borrower set |
| **Dates** | Note dates, first payment due date, closing date |
| **Payment History** | Last 12 months (date, amount, days late) |
| **Old Loan** | Rate, term, P&I, PITI, MIP (FHA) |
| **New Loan** | Rate, term, P&I, PITI, MIP (FHA) |
| **VA Specific** | Fees/costs for recoupment, funding fee |
| **Cash** | Cash-to-borrower amount |
| **Liens** | Subordinate lien information |
| **Disclosures** | Delivery timestamps |
| **Evidence** | Document-to-field mapping |
